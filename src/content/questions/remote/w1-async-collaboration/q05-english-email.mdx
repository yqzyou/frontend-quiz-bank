## 题目
你要给一个从未谋面的 client 发英文邮件请求确认技术方案。下列哪种开头最高效？

## 选项
A. "Hi John, I hope this email finds you well. As you may know, our team has been working on..."
B. "Hi John, **TL;DR: we recommend Postgres over MongoDB for the user-table workload. Need your +1 by Fri to land it in this sprint.** Details below." ✓
C. "Dear Mr. Smith, I am writing to you regarding the matter of..."
D. "Hey, quick question — what do you think?"

## 解析
**BLUF 原则：Bottom Line Up Front**（结论先行）

远程异步沟通里，收件人第一眼要看到：
1. 这是什么事？
2. 我需要做什么？
3. 截止时间是什么时候？

之后再展开背景、备选、风险。

**英文邮件结构**：

```
Subject: [Action needed] DB choice for user table — need +1 by Fri Aug 12

Hi John,

BLUF / TL;DR: We recommend Postgres. Need your +1 by Friday EOD
Pacific to keep sprint on track.

Context (3 paragraphs max):
- We're shipping the user-profile feature in week 8
- Original spec said MongoDB, but our read patterns are relational
- Benchmarks attached: Postgres 4× faster on the 3 hot queries

Options considered:
A. MongoDB (status quo) — flexible schema, but joins are slow
B. Postgres (recommend) — ACID, simpler queries, our team has ops experience
C. SQLite — too small for the write volume

Risk if we don't decide by Fri:
- Sprint slips 1 week; cascading impact on Q3 roadmap

Reply with: "go Postgres" / "go Mongo" / "let's chat — I'll book 15min"

Thanks,
Yasser
```

**为什么 B 最好？**
- 收件人看完 TL;DR 就能 +1 / 反对 / 提议聊
- 不需要读完 5 段才知道该做什么
- 时间压力（"by Fri EOD"）明确

**为什么 A 是反模式？**
- "I hope this email finds you well" 是中式套话直译，英文母语者觉得啰嗦
- 5 句开场后才进主题 → reviewer 失去耐心

**为什么 C 错？**
- "Dear Mr. Smith" 太正式，除非法律函
- "the matter of" 是中英直译，英美人不这么说

**为什么 D 错？**
- 没有上下文，对方需要追问 → 一来一回浪费时间
- "Hey" 在正式客户场景显得过于随意
